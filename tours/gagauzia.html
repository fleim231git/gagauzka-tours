Tasty Gagauzia Tour

⏱ 8 hours 👤 Guide: Masha 🚗 Transport included 🎫 All tickets & lunch included

About This Tour

Discover Gagauzia — a hidden autonomous region in the south of Moldova where Turkic traditions, Soviet heritage, and Moldovan hospitality blend into a world few outsiders ever see. Walk through the capital Comrat, explore a local market, visit a winery, and end the day with a visit to a local family.

This is not a standard sightseeing tour — it's a full 8-hour immersion into the daily life, traditions, and stories of one of Europe's most fascinating hidden communities.

Main Stops

  • Manuc Bey Mansion

    Restored noble estate with palaces, towers, and historic park areas.

  • Comrat City

    Guided experience in Gagauzia's capital — main square, cultural landmarks, city identity.

  • Local Market

    Authentic local market with seasonal produce, handmade goods, and everyday Gagauz life.

  • Winery Optional +30€/pp

    Winery visit with guided cellar tour and traditional wine tasting.

  • Beshalma Museum

    Gagauz Ethnographic Museum with a cinema session about traditional wedding customs.

  • Congaz Village

    One of the largest Gagauz villages — cultural excursion and traditional lunch experience.

  • Ceadîr-Lunga

    Local town with Soviet collection and unique cultural atmosphere.

  • Visit to local family + Master class Optional +20€/pp

    Cook alongside a Gagauz family, try on national costumes, taste homemade dishes.

  • Soviet Collection

    Unique private collection of Soviet-era objects, memorabilia, and everyday artifacts.
