Transnistria · PMR

Back to USSR Tour

⏱ Full day 👤 Guide: Masha 🚗 Transport included 🎫 Tickets · Lunch · Wine tasting included

About This Tour

Cross the border into Transnistria — a breakaway state where the Soviet Union never ended. Walk streets lined with Lenin statues, visit a fortress, explore a bottle museum, and taste wine from local cellars. Time stands still here — and Masha will bring it all to life.

One of the most unique experiences in Eastern Europe — a living museum of Soviet life, complete with its own currency, flag, and border checkpoints.

Main Stops

  • Bender Fortress

    Historic 16th-century fortress on the Dniester River — Ottoman and Eastern European heritage.

  • Tiraspol Walking Tour

    Main Square, government buildings, Soviet monuments, and central urban landmarks.

  • Green Market

    Local market full of Soviet-era goods, fresh produce, and everyday Transnistrian life.

  • Soviet-Themed Lunch

    Back to USSR Restaurant or authentic Soviet Stolovka — full nostalgic dining experience.

  • Bottle Museum in Ternovka

    Private museum with over 10,000 bottles from different countries and eras — truly unique.

  • Wine Tasting

    Tasting of local Transnistrian wines in a traditional cellar setting.

  • Ciobruci Village Optional +50€

    Preserved Soviet-era village with House of Culture and Lenin monument.

Masha

Lead Guide · Soviet History Specialist
