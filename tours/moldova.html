About This Tour

An immersive day through the heart of Moldova — from world-famous underground wineries to ancient monasteries carved into cliffs and picturesque villages frozen in time. This tour captures the soul of the country in a single day.

You'll visit Cricova Winery, one of the most famous wineries in the world, explore the dramatic Orheiul Vechi archaeological complex, walk through the traditional village of Butuceni, and enjoy authentic Moldovan countryside cuisine with panoramic valley views. Wine tasting, tickets, and lunch are all included.

Main Stops

  • Cricova Winery

    Underground wine city with guided cellar tour and premium tasting — one of the world's most celebrated wineries.

  • Orheiul Vechi Archaeological Complex

    Dramatic limestone cliffs, river canyon, cave monastery, and ancient settlement remains.

  • Butuceni Village Walk

    Traditional village with preserved rural architecture and ethnographic museum.

  • Curchi Monastery

    Beautiful baroque monastery with white stone buildings and peaceful natural surroundings.

  • Traditional Moldovan Lunch

    Countryside cuisine at Eco Resort Butuceni with panoramic views over the valley — included in the tour.

  • Wine Tasting

    Curated selection of Moldova's finest wines — included as part of the Cricova experience.
